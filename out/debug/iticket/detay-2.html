Description

TAEMIN, K-POP’S ICONIC STAR, IN ISTANBUL ON NOVEMBER 27!

TAEMIN, one of the most impressive performers in the K-Pop world, is coming to Ülker Sports and Event Hall on November 27 for an unforgettable concert experience!

TAEMIN Istanbul concert tickets go on sale October 23 at 16.00!

A member of the legendary K-Pop group SHINee and winner of millions of fans’ hearts worldwide with his successful solo career, TAEMIN is getting ready to meet his fans in Türkiye with his unique stage performance, mesmerizing choreography and powerful musical identity.

Having carved out a distinctive place on the K-Pop scene with hit songs such as “MOVE”, “WANT”, “Criminal”, “Advice” and “Guilty”, the artist proves through performances that bring music and dance together that he is not just a singer but a true stage icon.

Attracting great attention in the international arena and reaching wide audiences with his world tours, TAEMIN is now getting ready to bring the same excitement to Istanbul.

Get ready to witness TAEMIN’s mesmerizing world on this special night where music, dance and stagecraft come together!

Reserve your spot now to be a part of this unforgettable experience!

DETAILS ABOUT THE VIP PACKAGES ARE BELOW.

All VIP packages include a Front Stage concert ticket, and there is no need to purchase a separate concert ticket.

PLATINUM PACKAGE

Front Stage and early entry, soundcheck, send-off, signed printed poster, group photo with the artist (in groups of 10), signed exclusive photocard. Doors open at 16:30.

GOLD PACKAGE

Front Stage and early entry, soundcheck, send-off, signed printed poster, group photo with the artist (in groups of 10). Doors open at 16:30.

SILVER PACKAGE

Front Stage and early entry, soundcheck, send-off, signed printed poster. Doors open at 17:00.

TAEMIN Istanbul concert tickets are on sale at iTicket.com.tr.
