There is no admission of children under 6 for the hall and under 12 for the balconies. An accompanying adult is required for both. This restriction does not apply to special events geared for children.

As a principle, latecomers will be admitted only during the applause in the program. It is recommended that you arrive at the concert hall at least 15 minutes before the activity starts to avoid any distraction to the artists and audience.

Programme changes can be made in mandatory cases.

Ticket prices may vary periodically. Bilkent Symphony Orchestra reserves the right to increase ticket prices.
